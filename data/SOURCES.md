# Official importer documents to transcribe

These are the importer-hosted PDFs and pages that hold the real Israeli
per-km tables. They were located but could not be opened from the cloud
environment (network policy blocks the hosts). Open them from a normal
browser, transcribe the "טיפולים תקופתיים" table in our own words into the
matching schedule file, and move its `status` to `reviewed`.

## Toyota (יוניון מוטורס)
- Maintenance schedule download per model and year: https://www.toyota.co.il/owners/warranty
- Warranty and maintenance booklet: https://union-motors.toyota.co.il/files/15waranty
- Interval policy (15,000 km / 12 months for most models): https://www.toyota.co.il/company/news/service-and-maintenance
