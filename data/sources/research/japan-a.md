## Sources found (key discoveries)
- **Suzuki Israel (מכשירי תנועה)**: the book page https://suzuki.co.il/content/ספרי-נהג-סוזוקי has a Drupal exposed filter.
  `?field_title_value_selective=<model>` returns the Hebrew book for about 30 models, under https://suzuki.co.il/sites/default/files/pdf/ .
  All of them have a periodic-service table, so every Suzuki file is **reviewed**.
  Format: 2014+ books use 20,000 km columns up to 240k. Crossover-turbo up to chassis 650000 and Swift K12B use 15,000 km up to 180k. Old books (SX4, Alto, Splash, Jimny, Grand Vitara) use an importer table of 15,000 km to 90k.
- **Subaru Israel (יפנאוטו / סמלת)**: subaru.co.il is behind Incapsula. Playwright got the wp-json media list once, then was blocked.
  The books are in an open directory listing: https://samelet.com/ebooks/ (Subaru XV, Impreza, B4, Forester, Crosstrek, Outback, warranty booklets). "תכנית תחזוקה" is in chapter 11, in 15,000 km columns to 120k.
- **Mitsubishi (כלמוביל)**: I rechecked all Hebrew books, including 3 not downloaded before (Space Star 2024, ASX, Eclipse Cross, from mitsu-media.json). None has a maintenance table except Outlander 2021+.
  mitsu-tipulim-2024.pdf is the warranty certificate. I used **Mitsubishi Motors Australia** maintenance-schedule PDFs (all listed at https://www.mitsubishi-motors.com.au/owners/service/maintenance-schedule.html), in 15,000 km/12 m grids, status draft.
- **Isuzu (יוניברסל מוטורס)**: isuzu.co.il returns 403. I used Isuzu UTE Australia D-MAX 23MY owner's manual (RG, RZ4E) and Isuzu UK D-Max 17MY owner's manual (RT, 4JK1). Both drafts.

## Notes on transcription
- Suzuki tables were parsed from the PDF text layer with coordinates, using scratchpad `jpa_heb.py` / `jpa_suz.py`. I checked them against page images for S-Cross MC25, Crossover (all versions), Vitara 1600, Jimny new and Swift K12B. Books with a broken text layer (cd_crossover, vitara1600, alto, sx4, splash, jimny old, grand vitara, Subaru impreza/b4) were transcribed by hand from rendered page images.
- Some book oddities were copied as printed:
  - Celerio: PCV check at 80k and 200k.
  - Vitara 1600 and Super Swift: a stray parking-brake mark in the 140k column. I ignored it because the text says "first 20,000 only".
- Suzuki "manual gearbox oil (non-genuine) R every 40k" rows were mapped to inspect every 40k with a note. Clutch fluid (same reservoir as brake fluid) was not listed separately. Wheel bearings are merged into suspension notes.
- Subaru "(מ)" (recommended) drive-belt / wheel-bearing items are entered as inspect. Cabin filter "every 12 months or 12,000 km" is in long_interval.
